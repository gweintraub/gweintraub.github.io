This was an idea sitting in the back of my mind for a long time. I've been passionate about tools for as long as I can remember, and I've been exploring tools as sculptural objects as far back as my first semester in college. I've also always been over-dedicated to going well out of my way for a good pun. The goal here was to create a mostly-functional tool. To that end, the shaft is bent stainless steel with a forged and ground tip. The handle itself I bought off the shelf &emdash; my attempts to print my own handles were disappointing &emdash; but the "SCREW U" marking is laser etched, and the internal threading was cut on a CNC lathe. There's also a 3D-printed spacer inside the handle where the shaft enters. It tickles me that this piece incorporates everything from traditional forging to modern printing and machining processes; the simplicity of the design belies the complexity of the fabrication.
